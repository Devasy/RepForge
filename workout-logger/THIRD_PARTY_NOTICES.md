# Body diagram geometry

Muscle outlines are derived from MuscleMap by Melih Colpan (https://github.com/melihcolpan/MuscleMap), via openGym's body-paths.js at commit f049465c1e4732c84e7077286dc99a66fdd2ed23 (https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym).
Only the MIT-licensed geometry is reused. The Flutter renderer and RepForge muscle mapping are implemented for RepForge.
